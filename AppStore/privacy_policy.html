Privacy Policy

Forio — AI CV & Cover Letter Builder · Suftnet Ltd · Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Forio is built by Suftnet Ltd. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Forio stores the following data locally on your device using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device except as described below:

  • Your profile: name, contact details, work experience, education, skills
  • Job applications: job descriptions you have entered
  • Generated CVs and cover letters
  • App preferences and settings

Data sent to third parties

OpenAI (GPT-4o): When you generate a CV, your profile data and the job description are sent to OpenAI's API to generate the CV and cover letter. This data is used solely for generation and is subject to OpenAI's privacy policy. We do not store your data on OpenAI servers beyond the duration of the API call.

RevenueCat: If you purchase Forio Premium, your purchase is processed via RevenueCat and Apple's App Store. RevenueCat receives a random device identifier for purchase verification. No personal profile data is shared. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not sell data to third parties
  • We do not store your CV data on our servers

Camera

Forio requests camera access only when you choose to scan a CV or job description. Camera images are processed locally by iOS (VisionKit) and sent to OpenAI only for the purpose of text extraction. Images are not stored or shared beyond this purpose.

Children

Forio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes
